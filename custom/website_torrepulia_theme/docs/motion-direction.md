# Torrepulia Motion Direction

MOTION_INTENSITY: 7 / 10, requested by the owner.
Apply Emil Kowalski's transform/opacity, interruptibility and reduced-motion rules.

The reading sequence is earth, family, Peranzana olives and bottle, then table
and shopping. There is no persistent moving oil mark. Three local accents:
a short line above the family heading, an underline beneath Peranzana on the
bottle heading, and a small flat gold drop before the formats with the message
"Dalla nostra terra, alla tua tavola." Lines draw as their headings enter view.

The Torremaggiore panoramic photograph uses a continuous subtle zoom from 1 to
1.025 and back, taking 12 seconds per leg. Its pause button belongs to the photo;
pause outside the viewport and honor reduced motion. Territory copy and map now
share a pale unframed section, with the photograph below them.

The homepage hero is now static and full-screen, with the provided photograph
"Olio Torrepulia tra foglie d'ulivo_16-9.png". No image or copy entrance animation
and no pause button. The territory uses a one-time geographic reveal of the
ISTAT-derived Puglia outline and the Torremaggiore point. Its panoramic photograph
retains the existing 2.5% zoom with a pause button attached to the photograph.

The bottle enters with a 100px lift and a seven-degree planar tilt, then settles.

The family chapter is a compact editorial grid: heading and short story on the
left, today's authentic photograph on the right and a smaller archive offset
below its left edge. Captions remain outside the photographs. A three-name
lineage connects the generations without adding another gold accent. Photos
enter once with an 800ms ease-out and 120ms stagger; no continuous photo motion.
On mobile, heading, photographs and story stack in reading order.
Do not simulate a full 3D rotation from one photograph. Keep label proportions.
The fidelity WebP is a tight crop of the original photograph; browser exposure
correction improves display but does not restore missing photographic detail.
Generated alternatives were rejected because their labels differed.

Reference consulted: https://getdesign.md/framer/design-md from Awesome DESIGN.md.
Borrow only the hierarchy of photographic motion and stable interaction surfaces,
not its colors, fonts, brand assets or composition.
